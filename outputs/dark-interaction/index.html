A place to begin

A computer that meets you where you are.

We want everyday work to feel lighter. A thought should have somewhere to go, and the things you need should feel close at hand.

Start with the person

Speaking, typing and pointing are equally natural ways to ask. The system should understand what we mean, show what it is doing, and leave us in control.

Make room for the work

Quiet surfaces. Readable words. A workspace that holds the thread without demanding our attention.

Less to manage.
More room to think.
